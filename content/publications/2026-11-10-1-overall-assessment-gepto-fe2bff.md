### 3. Content‑Specific Suggestions  

#### 3.1 Clarify Terminology  
- **“Neo‑Liberal Orthodoxy”** – Define whether you refer to the post‑Cold‑War consensus (IMF/World Bank structural adjustment) or a broader ideological strand.  
- **“Dynamic Taxation”** – Explain how the transaction‑tax interacts with existing financial‑market regulations to avoid double‑taxation concerns.

#### 3.2 Strengthen Empirical Claims  
- When stating “Nordic model… Gini ≈ 0.25,” cite the latest OECD inequality data (2023).  
- For the 2008 and 2023 crises, reference specific scholarly analyses (e.g., Brunnermeier 2019; IMF 2024 Crisis Report).

#### 3.3 Expand on the **Ecological Ledger (EL)**  
- Detail the measurement methodology (e.g., life‑cycle assessment, carbon accounting standards such as the GHG Protocol).  
- Discuss potential integration with existing schemes like the EU Emissions Trading System.

#### 3.4 Address Potential Objections  
- **Political Feasibility:** Anticipate resistance from entrenched financial interests and outline a coalition‑building strategy (e.g., alliances with labour unions, green NGOs).  
- **Moral Hazard of UBE:** Provide empirical evidence from pilot UBI experiments (Finland 2017‑18, Kenya 2022‑24) showing labour‑market effects.

#### 3.5 Ethical Foundations – Cite Primary Sources  
- When invoking **Zarathushtra’s righteousness**, reference the *Gathas* (e.g., Yasna 1.1).  
- For **Solon’s seisachtheia**, cite the *Constitution of the Athenians* (Pseudo‑Xenophon).  
- Include brief quotations to anchor the moral arguments.
